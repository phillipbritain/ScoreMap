# Scenario games are where real ones are

Scenarios (ADR-0009) put any configured league's game at any venue in their list: Lakers v Celtics at Wembley, an NHL game at Wankhede Stadium. The venue list was a list of places, chosen to put pins on every continent, and about half of it hosted no league ScoreMap shows (EFL grounds around London, stadiums in Brazil, Japan, Australia and Africa). A scenario game's venue also carried no ESPN id, so it never got ESPN's photo, and the panel of a game run locally or in a demo looked unlike a real one. The owner wanted the games run in development and demos to look like real ones, as the welcome tour's now do (ADR-0027).

So each venue in the list is a real venue of a configured league, with the leagues that really play there. For each, it has the venue as that league's scoreboard gives it (ESPN's id, and the city, region and country as the league writes them: MLS puts the state in the city, college basketball gives no country) and the league's teams whose home it is. A game there is in one of those leagues, at home to one of its teams there, against another of the league's teams; at a neutral site (a World Cup ground) any two of the league's teams can play. The venue goes out as a real game's would, so the venue photo search, the venue locator and the game panel get what they'd get from ESPN.

## Considered options

- **IDs only, keeping any game at any venue**: each venue would have the ESPN id of its main league, and games in that league would get ESPN's photo. Smaller, but an NHL game could still be at Wankhede Stadium, and ESPN's ids are a league's own (Soldier Field is 3933 in the NFL, 1452 in MLS).
- **Dropping the venues no configured league plays at, and shrinking the groups**, rather than replacing them: crowded fills 40 games from its group, and play needs free venues to bring new ones on at.
- **Field events where they really happen** (F1 at Suzuka, the Australian Open, golf worldwide), to keep pins on every continent: fill makes only two-sided games today, so this was left for later.

## Consequences

- The venue list is built from ESPN: the home venue of each team in the team list (its usual ground over its recent home games, leaving out neutral sites, and for a college baseball, softball or hockey team only one in its school's state, since early-season tournaments are listed as home games), the 2026 World Cup's grounds as neutral sites, and the venues of the field events written out. ESPN's API gave no past IPL games to read them from, so the IPL grounds have no id, and their names are the grounds' own rather than ESPN's. College baseball's and softball's venues are 2025's: ESPN's 2026 scoreboards gave those games no venue at all.
- Pins leave Japan, Brazil, Australia and Africa: the configured leagues reach only North America, Europe and India (and a Champions League ground in Azerbaijan or Kazakhstan). Scenario pins are where real games' are.
- Groups are worked out from where each venue is: `new-york`, `los-angeles`, `london` and `london-and-nearby` by distance from the city's center, `big-cities` within 40 km of the world's biggest cities that have a configured league's venue, `us-college` every venue of a college league, `worldwide` every venue of a professional or international league, and `written-out` the venues only the written-out games use (race tracks, golf courses, tennis tournaments and the like).
- A fill or play passes over a venue whose home teams are all already playing, for the next one that can have a game. A written-out game must be at a venue where its league plays, at home to its team there, so a scenario file can't put one where no real game would be.
- The venue list is also where the venue photo search finds a venue's ids in other leagues: a live, scenario or tour game whose league has no ESPN photo of its venue takes another league's there (MLS's Soldier Field has none, and the NFL's does). It ships to production, as the worldwide scenario needs it (ADR-0019); a venue it doesn't list gets only its own league's photo or Commons'.
- The venue list, its lookups and the team list must agree: a venue's home teams are in the team list, or it never gets a game. The list was built once from ESPN with a throwaway script, not one kept in the repo; when the team list changes, its venues are added the same way, by hand.
